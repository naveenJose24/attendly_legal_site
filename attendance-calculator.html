How many classes can I miss?

Enter the classes you attended and the classes held so far. You’ll see your attendance percentage, how many classes you can still miss, or how many you need to attend in a row to reach your target.

75%

How the attendance calculation works

Attendance % = classes attended ÷ classes held × 100. If you attended 45 of 60 classes, your attendance is 75%.

Classes you can miss

If you are above your target, the number of future classes you can skip is m = floor(A ÷ T − H), where A is classes attended, H is classes held and T is your target as a decimal (0.75 for 75%). Example: 50 of 60 attended → floor(50 ÷ 0.75 − 60) = 6 classes.

Classes you need to attend

If you are below your target, attend n = ceil((T × H − A) ÷ (1 − T)) classes in a row. Example: 40 of 60 attended → ceil((45 − 40) ÷ 0.25) = 20 classes.

Rules vary. Some institutions count labs, tutorials or approved leave differently. Use this as a planning estimate and confirm the official rule for your course.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my attendance percentage?

Divide the classes you attended by the total classes held, then multiply by 100. For example, 45 attended out of 60 held is 75%.

How many classes can I miss and still keep 75%?

Enter your numbers above with a 75% target. The calculator uses m = floor(A ÷ 0.75 − H). With 50 attended out of 60, you can miss 6 more classes.

How many classes do I need to attend to reach 75%?

The calculator uses n = ceil((0.75 × H − A) ÷ 0.25). With 40 attended out of 60, you need to attend the next 20 classes.

Can I use a target other than 75%?

Yes. Change the target to 60%, 80%, 85% or whatever your institution requires.

Should I track each subject separately?

Usually, yes. Many colleges apply the minimum per subject, so one weak subject can matter even if your overall average looks fine. Read how to track attendance by subject.

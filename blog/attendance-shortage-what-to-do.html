Attendance Shortage: What It Means and What to Do Next

Understand attendance shortage, check the rules that apply to you, and make a practical recovery plan without panic.

Updated September 2026 · Attendly guide

The practical answer

Attendance shortage means your recorded attendance is below the minimum set by your school or institution for a course or term.

Check the exact rule first

There is no universal shortage threshold. Some institutions calculate attendance by subject, some include labs separately, and some allow approved exceptions.

Find the official rule, confirm the reporting period, and ask your department whether corrected records or approved leave can change your total.

Make the next step measurable

Calculate your current percentage, count the remaining scheduled classes, and identify how many future classes you must attend to reach your target.

A simple record of every class makes the conversation easier if your official portal and your own notes disagree. Keep copies of relevant approvals and communicate early.

Check your institution’s version.

Thresholds, approved absences, and exam eligibility can vary. Use this guide to frame the question, then confirm the answer in your official course or institution policy.

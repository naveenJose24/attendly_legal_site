Present vs. Absent: How Attendance Percentages Actually Work

See how present and absent records become an attendance percentage, including why hours and sessions should not be mixed.

Updated September 2026 · Attendly guide

The calculation

Attendance percentage is the attended portion of the total conducted amount: present sessions or hours divided by present plus absent sessions or hours.

Keep the unit consistent

If your institution records hours, calculate with hours. If it records sessions, calculate with sessions. Mixing one two-hour lab with one lecture session can distort the result.

The total conducted amount is usually present plus absent, but confirm how cancelled, excused, or rescheduled classes are handled.

Use the history to find patterns

A percentage tells you where you are; the record shows why. Look for days, subjects, or time slots where absences repeat.

A short note beside a record can help you distinguish a one-off event from a scheduling problem.

Check your institution’s version.

Thresholds, approved absences, and exam eligibility can vary. Use this guide to frame the question, then confirm the answer in your official course or institution policy.

How Many Classes Can I Miss and Still Stay Above 75%?

Use the attendance formula to estimate how many future classes you can miss while staying above a 75% target.

Updated September 2026 · Attendly guide

The calculation

The answer depends on your attended total, classes already held, remaining schedule, and your institution’s required percentage.

Start with your current totals

Let A be classes attended and H be classes held. To stay at or above a target T, future misses m must satisfy A ÷ (H + m) ≥ T.

At 75%, a student with 60 attended out of 80 held is exactly on target. Another absence would lower the result, so the safe estimate is zero additional misses unless future attended classes rebuild the buffer.

Treat the result as a planning estimate

The calculation is only as reliable as the totals and policy you enter. Labs, tutorials, approved leave, and subject-level rules may be counted differently.

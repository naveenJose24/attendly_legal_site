Tracking

The Easiest Way to Track Attendance by Subject

Learn why subject-level tracking is clearer than one overall number and how to build a simple subject attendance routine.

Updated September 2026 · Attendly guide

The useful setup

Track each subject with its own classes held, classes attended, target, and recent trend. An overall average can hide a subject that needs attention.

Use one small record per subject

For every subject, keep the class date, status, and hours or sessions. Add the target percentage that applies to that subject if it differs from your general target.

This makes it easier to see whether a low overall number comes from one course, a lab schedule, or a pattern across the whole week.

Review the exceptions first

Sort your attention toward subjects close to the minimum, not only the ones with the lowest number. A course with fewer remaining classes may need action sooner.

Attendly’s subject summaries are designed to keep this comparison readable at a glance.

Check your institution’s version.

Thresholds, approved absences, and exam eligibility can vary. Use this guide to frame the question, then confirm the answer in your official course or institution policy.

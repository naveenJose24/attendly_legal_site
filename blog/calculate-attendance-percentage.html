How to Calculate Your Attendance Percentage

Learn the simple attendance percentage formula, see a worked example, and understand what your result means for the semester.

Updated September 2026 · Attendly guide

The calculation

Attendance percentage is calculated by dividing the classes or hours you attended by the total classes or hours held, then multiplying by 100.

The attendance percentage formula

Use this formula: (classes attended ÷ classes held) × 100. If you track hours instead of class meetings, use present hours and conducted hours consistently.

For example, 42 attended classes out of 50 held gives (42 ÷ 50) × 100 = 84%. Keep the denominator tied to what your institution records.

Why the number can change quickly

One missed class has a bigger effect early in a semester because fewer classes have been counted. Later, each single class usually changes the percentage less.

Track each subject separately when your institution calculates eligibility per course. Attendly helps you keep the inputs visible instead of rebuilding the calculation from memory.

Check your institution’s version.

Thresholds, approved absences, and exam eligibility can vary. Use this guide to frame the question, then confirm the answer in your official course or institution policy.

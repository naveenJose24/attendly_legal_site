Tracking

How to Track Attendance Across a Full Semester

Build a semester-long attendance tracking habit with subject records, weekly reviews, and a simple target buffer.

Updated September 2026 · Attendly guide

The useful setup

A semester tracker works best when it records each class close to the time it happens and gives you a weekly summary by subject.

Set up before the first week

List each subject, its schedule, target, and whether it uses sessions or hours. Start with a clean baseline so the first percentage is meaningful.

If your timetable changes, update the schedule rather than trying to remember the change later.

Review once a week

A five-minute weekly review is enough to spot a subject trending toward the minimum. Compare current attendance with the number of classes left, not just the semester average.

Attendly keeps semester records and subject summaries together so the review can stay small.

Check your institution’s version.

Thresholds, approved absences, and exam eligibility can vary. Use this guide to frame the question, then confirm the answer in your official course or institution policy.

Worth doing next

Keep one clear record.

Record the subject, the date, and the result while the details are still easy to remember.

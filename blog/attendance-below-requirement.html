What Happens When Your Attendance Falls Below the Requirement?

What to check when attendance drops below a course requirement, how to calculate your path back, and when to ask for help.

Updated September 2026 · Attendly guide

Before you decide

The next step depends on your institution. You may need to contact a department, submit documentation, attend remaining classes, or follow an appeal process.

Do not guess the consequence

Below-requirement outcomes can range from a warning to restricted assessment eligibility, but the actual rule belongs to your institution and course.

Check the handbook or official portal, then ask the relevant office what deadline, evidence, or correction process applies.

Bring clear numbers

Write down classes held, attended, absent, target, and remaining schedule. A clear record helps you explain the situation and identify whether a data correction is needed.

Keep attending scheduled classes while you confirm the process unless an official instruction says otherwise.

Check your institution’s version.

Thresholds, approved absences, and exam eligibility can vary. Use this guide to frame the question, then confirm the answer in your official course or institution policy.

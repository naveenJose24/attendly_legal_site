Attendance Calculator: Current Percentage, Classes Needed, and Classes You Can Miss

Understand the three attendance numbers students usually need: current percentage, classes needed to recover, and future classes they can miss.

Updated September 2026 · Attendly guide

The calculation

A useful attendance calculator should show your current percentage, the classes you need to attend to reach your target, and the classes you can miss without falling below it.

The three numbers to calculate

Current percentage is attended divided by held. Classes needed to reach a target solve for the smallest future attendance streak that brings you up. Classes you can miss solve for the largest future absence that keeps you above the target.

Each result depends on accurate subject totals and the number of classes still scheduled.

Do not confuse estimates with permission

A calculator cannot know whether your institution counts late arrivals, approved leave, labs, or makeup sessions differently. It only works with the assumptions you enter.

Use the result to ask a better question of your official policy or advisor, not to override it.

Check your institution’s version.

Thresholds, approved absences, and exam eligibility can vary. Use this guide to frame the question, then confirm the answer in your official course or institution policy.

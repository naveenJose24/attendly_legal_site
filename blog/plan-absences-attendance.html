Planning

How to Plan Absences Without Losing Attendance Eligibility

A practical way to think through planned absences, remaining classes, and your institution’s attendance requirement.

Updated September 2026 · Attendly guide

A way to think about it

Before planning an absence, calculate its effect on the relevant subject and confirm that approved leave or special rules apply as expected.

Run the numbers before the date

Use the subject’s current attended and held totals, then model the absence as one more class in the denominator. If the result is close to the minimum, the plan may leave no room for later disruption.

Remember that a calculator cannot decide whether an absence is approved or how your institution records it.

Make a backup plan

Check notes, recordings, transport, and any required makeup work before the absence. If the event is unavoidable, communicate early and keep documentation where appropriate.

The best plan protects both attendance and your ability to catch up academically.

Check your institution’s version.

Thresholds, approved absences, and exam eligibility can vary. Use this guide to frame the question, then confirm the answer in your official course or institution policy.

Worth doing next

Look at the remaining classes.

A simple count of what is left usually makes the decision less abstract.

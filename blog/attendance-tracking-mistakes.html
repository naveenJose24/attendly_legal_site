Tracking

Common Attendance-Tracking Mistakes Students Make

Avoid the most common attendance-tracking errors, from mixing hours and sessions to forgetting subject-level rules.

Updated September 2026 · Attendly guide

The useful setup

The most common mistakes are recording from memory, mixing units, using one overall average, and treating an online calculator as an official policy.

Errors that change the math

Mixing class sessions with hours can produce a misleading percentage. So can counting cancelled classes as conducted or forgetting to update a corrected record.

Use one consistent unit per subject and keep the original date for each entry.

Errors that change the decision

An overall average can hide a subject shortage. A percentage from a generic calculator cannot confirm exam eligibility. And a target copied from a friend may not apply to your course.

Keep your record clear, then verify policy questions with the official source.

Check your institution’s version.

Thresholds, approved absences, and exam eligibility can vary. Use this guide to frame the question, then confirm the answer in your official course or institution policy.

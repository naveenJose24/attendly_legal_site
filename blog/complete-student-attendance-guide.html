The Complete Student Guide to Attendance Tracking

A practical guide to attendance formulas, subject tracking, recovery plans, safe targets, and weekly habits.

Updated September 2026 · Attendly guide

The short version

Good attendance tracking combines accurate records, subject-level percentages, a realistic target, and a short weekly review.

Start with accurate records

Record each class or hour as close to the event as possible. Keep units consistent, separate subjects when rules differ, and note corrections or approved exceptions.

Then calculate the current percentage using attended divided by conducted totals.

Use the numbers to plan

Model how many future classes you need to reach a target and how much room remains for absence. Treat the output as an estimate until your institution confirms its rules.

Review once a week, protect the next class, and ask for support early when a barrier is bigger than a reminder can solve.

Check your institution’s version.

Thresholds, approved absences, and exam eligibility can vary. Use this guide to frame the question, then confirm the answer in your official course or institution policy.

Worth doing next

Start with one subject.

You do not need a perfect system on day one. Begin with the course you are checking today.

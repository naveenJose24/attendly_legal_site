What Attendance Percentage Do You Need for College?

A practical guide to attendance requirements, why they vary, and how to find the rule that applies to your course.

Updated September 2026 · Attendly guide

Before you decide

The required attendance percentage depends on your college, course, country, and department. There is no single rule that applies everywhere.

Why percentages differ

Institutions may set different minimums for lectures, labs, clinical sessions, or professional programs. Some also describe approved leave or condonation separately.

A number you see online can be a useful example, but it should never replace your handbook, student portal, or department guidance.

Build a safe personal target

Once you know the official minimum, choose a buffer above it. A target with room for illness, travel, and unexpected events is easier to manage than aiming at the exact minimum.

Track by subject and review weekly so a small dip is visible while you still have time to respond.

Check your institution’s version.

Thresholds, approved absences, and exam eligibility can vary. Use this guide to frame the question, then confirm the answer in your official course or institution policy.

How to Build a Simple Weekly Attendance Routine

A five-minute weekly system for checking attendance, spotting risk early, and starting each class day prepared.

Updated September 2026 · Attendly guide

A workable starting point

A useful weekly attendance routine has three moments: prepare the schedule, record classes, and review subject trends before the next week begins.

Prepare on the weekend

Look over the week’s timetable, travel time, assessments, and any known conflicts. Put the classes that are hardest to reach at the top of your planning attention.

Pack materials and set realistic reminders; the goal is fewer last-minute decisions.

Close the loop after class

Record present or absent status while it is still fresh. At the end of the week, check subject percentages and write one next action for anything close to target.

The routine should take minutes, not become another large assignment.

Check your institution’s version.

Thresholds, approved absences, and exam eligibility can vary. Use this guide to frame the question, then confirm the answer in your official course or institution policy.

Worth doing next

Make the next check small.

A record becomes useful when it is easy to update after an ordinary class day.

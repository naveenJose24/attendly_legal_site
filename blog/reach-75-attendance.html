How Many Classes Do I Need to Attend to Reach 75%?

Learn how to calculate the minimum future attendance streak needed to move from your current percentage to a 75% target.

Updated September 2026 · Attendly guide

The practical answer

Solve A + n divided by H + n ≥ 0.75, where A is classes attended, H is classes held, and n is future classes you attend.

The recovery calculation

For a 75% target, solve (A + n) ÷ (H + n) ≥ 0.75. Round n up to the next whole class because you cannot attend part of a class.

Example: with 54 attended out of 80 held, you need enough future attended classes to make the new ratio at least 75%. A calculator can do the arithmetic, while your timetable tells you whether the recovery is realistic.

Protect the recovery streak

Once you know the required number, mark those classes in your schedule and remove avoidable conflicts. Recheck after each week because one absence changes the remaining path.

Confirm the target and counting method with your institution before relying on the estimate.

Check your institution’s version.

Thresholds, approved absences, and exam eligibility can vary. Use this guide to frame the question, then confirm the answer in your official course or institution policy.

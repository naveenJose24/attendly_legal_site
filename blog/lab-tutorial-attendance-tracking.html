Attendance Tracking for Lab Classes and Tutorials

How to track labs and tutorials accurately when they have different hours, schedules, or attendance rules from lectures.

Updated September 2026 · Attendly guide

The useful setup

Track labs and tutorials as their own course activity when they have different sessions, hours, or eligibility rules.

Record the activity that was actually held

A lab may count by hours while a lecture counts by sessions. Record the unit your institution uses and keep the activity name specific enough to distinguish sections.

Include makeup sessions or cancelled periods according to the official record, not assumptions.

Check whether rules are separate

Some programs require minimum attendance in both lecture and practical components. Others combine them. Read the course outline and ask early if the wording is unclear.

Separate records make it easier to spot a practical-session shortage before it surprises you.

Check your institution’s version.

Thresholds, approved absences, and exam eligibility can vary. Use this guide to frame the question, then confirm the answer in your official course or institution policy.

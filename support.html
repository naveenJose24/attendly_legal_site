Frequently asked questions

How do I add a semester?

Open Settings, choose the semester area, and create a semester with its name, dates, and target percentage. Your active semester target is used across the app.

How is my attendance percentage calculated?

Attendly calculates present hours divided by conducted hours, where conducted hours are present hours plus absent hours. If you have no records, the percentage is 0%.

Can I record more than one class on the same day?

Yes. You can add multiple attendance records for the same subject and date, including different class hours and optional notes.

How do I delete an attendance record?

Open the relevant subject or history view, select the record, and use the delete action. Deleting a subject also deletes its attendance records.

Can I request a feature?

Absolutely. Send your idea to support@attendly.app. Clear examples of your workflow help us understand what to improve.

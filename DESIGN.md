# Attendly Legal & Marketing Site

## Direction

An editorial academic utility site: calm enough for legal reading, warm enough to feel like a student product. The supplied Attendly logo is the primary brand mark and appears in the shared navigation and footer.

## Tokens

- Ink: `#17152b`
- Indigo: `#4f46e5`
- Green: `#17ad72`
- Paper: `#fbfbfd`
- Lavender wash: `#efefff`
- Rule: `#e7e7ef`
- Display: Fraunces
- Body: Lexend

## Signature

The landing page uses a split “attendance ledger” hero: a large, quiet statement on the left and a floating attendance card on the right. The green check arc echoes the exact logo without recreating or altering it.

You’re in control

Request account deletion.

You can ask us to delete your Attendly account and associated data at any time.

What happens next

Send an email from the address connected to your account. We’ll confirm the request, verify ownership when necessary, and process deletion of the account and associated data.

Deletion is permanent. Your subjects, semesters, and attendance records cannot be restored after the request is completed.

How to request deletion

  1. Open your email app.
  2. Send a message to appgenieteam@gmail.com.
  3. Use the subject line “Attendly account deletion request”.
  4. Include the email address associated with your account. Do not include your password.

Need help first?

If you only need to remove a subject or attendance record, you can do that inside Attendly without deleting your full account. Visit Support if you need help.

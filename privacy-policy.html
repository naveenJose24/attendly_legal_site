Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Overview

Attendly is an attendance tracker designed to help students organize subjects, semesters, and class records. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information you add

Depending on the features you use, Attendly may store information you enter, such as semester names and dates, attendance targets, subject names and codes, attendance dates, class hours, attendance status, and optional notes.

How we use information

We use this information to provide the app’s core features, including calculating attendance percentages, showing subject summaries, displaying history, and helping you understand progress toward a target.

Storage and access

Attendly is designed to keep your attendance data available on your device for offline use. If future versions offer account or synchronization features, this policy will be updated before those features become active.

Attendly does not sell your personal information.

Data retention and deletion

Your records remain available until you delete them in the app or request account and data deletion. To make a deletion request, visit our Account Deletion page.

Children’s privacy

Attendly is intended for students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will show when the latest version was published.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to privacy@attendly.app.
